Brooklyn Public Library · Central Library, Grand Army Plaza

Aug 21 – Sep 12, 2026 · Free and open to the public

The first-ever exhibition of the Machel Montano collection.

Forty years of soca, shown for the very first time. Photographs, costumes, awards and memories, carefully preserved across four decades, tell the journey of the King of Soca: born in Trinidad and Tobago, carried through Brooklyn, and shared with the world. Presented in partnership with the Brooklyn Public Library at Central Library and across the Little Caribbean branches.

Inside the Exhibition

  • The Archive. Four decades of photographs, costumes and awards, first time on display.
  • Encore Vinyl Station. A dedicated listening station for the new album.
  • The Lyric Book. A five-volume, hand-bound book of Machel's lyrics, 1982–2026.
  • In Print. A wall of local newspapers and foreign magazines from before social media.
  • Interactive Media. Live performances and rare footage on screens throughout.
  • Little Caribbean. Free family programming across Flatbush, Crown Heights, Clarendon, Rugby and Brower Park.
