About

Machel Montano, the Collection and the Foundation.

Machel Montano

Hailing from Trinidad and Tobago, Machel Montano, known as the King of Soca, has been sharing his music with the world for more than four decades. Since a child, Machel has been a leader in the genre, winning countless awards and pushing soca music onto the international stage with growing velocity.

Machel is the winner of 12 Trinidad and Tobago Road Marches, the most ever, and 7 International Soca Monarch titles. He has collaborated and shared stages with some of the world's most renowned music icons, including Ariana Grande, Pitbull, Drake, Rihanna, Mighty Sparrow, Calypso Rose, Black Stalin, Mighty Shadow and Shaggy.

The Collection

The Collection is Machel Montano's personal archive: costumes, instruments, recordings, press, photographs, trophies and papers, kept from the beginning of his career in 1982. In 2024 it was inscribed on the UNESCO Memory of the World Register for Latin America and the Caribbean.

The Machel Montano Foundation for Greatness is its custodian. This exhibition draws on the Collection and is the first time a substantial part of it has been shown to the public.

The Foundation

The Machel Montano Foundation for Greatness is the philanthropic arm of the Montano family, founded by Machel Montano and co-led by his mother, Elizabeth Montano. It invests in young people across Trinidad and Tobago through education, mentorship and training for emerging soca artists, and supports young athletes and health initiatives.

It also builds livelihoods in rural cocoa growing communities, supplying equipment, training and soft loans so that growers can make and sell their own chocolate.
