Programming

Every public event at Central Library and across the five Little Caribbean branches, in date order.

All events are free

  1. Sat 22 Aug11.00 am

    Central Library, Youth Wing

    Boy Boy storytime with Library staff.

  2. Mon 24 Aug7.00 pm

    Central Library

    Public opening: panel discussion, exhibition tour and listening party in the vinyl section.

  3. Tue 25 Aug11.00 am to 12.30 pm

    Brower Park

    Reading of Boy Boy and the Magic Drum, mask making, and a mini carnival parade with a live rhythm section.

  4. Thu 27 Aug11.00 am to 12.30 pm

    In conversation with Dr Ottley: Analysis of Machel's lyrics across the years.

  6. Tue 1 Sep3.00 pm

    Crown Heights

    Reading of Boy Boy and the Magic Drum, mask making, and a performance by three youth pannists.

  7. Wed 2 Sep3.00 to 5.00 pm

    Rugby

    Youth steel band and stilt walkers, reading of Boy Boy and the Magic Drum, youth dance, masquerade and carnival craft.

  8. Wed 2 Sep7.00 pm

    Central Library

    In conversation with Dr Ottley: Breakdown of cross-cultural collaborations.

  9. Thu 3 Sep4.30 to 6.30 pm

    Flatbush

    Carnival characters parade and rhythm section, reading of King of Soca by Lady Montano, and a soca performance.

  10. Thu 3 Sep7.00 pm

    Central Library plaza

    DJ set followed by an outdoor screening of the Like Ah Boss documentary on the library facade, from approximately 8.15 pm.

  11. Wed 9 Sep7.00 pm

    Central Library

    Closing program: a look into the 40-year biography of the King of Soca book with Lady Montano and Maxine Williams.
